01 The proposition

Not an object that plays music.
A ritual that makes time audible.

Most precious objects ask to be looked at. LARK asks for participation. Wind the crown. Let the lacquered petals part. Watch stored energy become movement, movement become air, and air become a private room of sound.

It performs no task. That is precisely its purpose.

05 The edition

Twelve will exist.
None will repeat.

Each LARK carries a unique nocturne, pinned by hand into its cylinder. The owner may commission the final passage with our composer: a remembered interval, a private cadence, a musical signature that belongs to one object alone.
